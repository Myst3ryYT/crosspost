Privacy Policy

Crosspost · Last updated October 12, 2026

Crosspost is a personal tool that runs on its developer's own computer and reposts their own TikTok videos to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to that information.

What Crosspost accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). Crosspost does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the connected Instagram professional account and to read that account's username and ID.
  • TikTok: only publicly available information about the developer's own TikTok videos (the video files, captions, cover images and view counts).

How it's used

This information is used only to upload the videos and captions the user selects and reviews, to the destinations the user selects. Crosspost does not use it for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Where it's stored

Sign-in tokens, the list of videos and the posting history are stored only on the user's own computer. Crosspost has no server and no database of its own, and the developer does not collect any of this data.

Sharing

Crosspost does not sell or share user data. Data leaves the user's computer only in these cases:

  • Videos and captions are sent to YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta) when the user chooses to post them.
  • Because Instagram's API requires a video to be available at a public web address, a video being posted to Instagram is temporarily uploaded to Litterbox (litterbox.catbox.moe), a file host that deletes it automatically after one hour. These are videos that are already public on TikTok.

YouTube API Services

Crosspost uses YouTube API Services. By using Crosspost with a YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke Crosspost's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings.
  • Revoke Instagram access in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
  • Disconnecting an account inside Crosspost deletes its saved sign-in from the computer. Deleting the Crosspost folder removes all stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
