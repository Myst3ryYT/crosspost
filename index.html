Crosspost

A personal tool that reposts my own TikTok videos to my YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

What it does

Crosspost runs on my own computer. It reads my public TikTok videos, lets me pick which ones to share and where, and uploads them to my own YouTube channel and Instagram account using the official YouTube Data API and Instagram API, with the title and caption I review before posting.

Who it's for

Crosspost is a private tool used only by its developer to manage their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
